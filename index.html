Keyboard shortcuts

Tools: V move · M marquee · T type · P pen · U shapes (Shift+U cycles) · F frame · R rotate · G guide · C crop · I eyedropper · H hand · Z zoom

Guides: drag from a ruler, or G then click/drag. Drag a guide back onto a ruler to delete it. View → Snap to Guides snaps moves to them.

Type: Ctrl+Shift+, / . size · Ctrl+U underline · Character panel weight + italic

Fill: Edit → Linear / Radial Gradient Fill on a selected vector (real Skia shader)

Edit: Ctrl+Z/Y undo/redo · Ctrl+J duplicate · Ctrl+G group · Del delete

File: Ctrl+N new · Ctrl+O projects · Ctrl+S save · Shift+Ctrl+P place

View: Ctrl+R rulers · Ctrl+= / − zoom · Ctrl+0 fit · Ctrl+1 100%
